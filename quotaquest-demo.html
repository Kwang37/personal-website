01 / The challenge

300 applications. Almost no responses.

Reviewing an early client's application history exposed a targeting problem that resume editing alone could not fix.

What the audit revealed

Of 300 applications, approximately 20% went to employers that explicitly excluded international students. Fewer than 150 roles were genuinely aligned with the candidate's profile.

The strategic shift

Rather than treating every application as equally valuable, we expanded the service beyond CV and cover-letter editing to targeted job selection and hands-on application support.

Product insight: Increasing application volume is not useful when the underlying eligibility and fit criteria are wrong.
02 / From insight to workflow

A more intentional application process.

STEP 01

Qualify opportunities

Identify eligibility constraints and assess role alignment before investing effort in an application.

STEP 02

Track the pipeline

Coordinate CV and cover-letter revisions, submit applications, and track each student’s progress. The earlier resume also describes a Python + Excel tracking workflow; its implementation ownership should be verified before attribution.

STEP 03

Review and iterate

Review application outcomes, adjust job targeting, and communicate next steps with students.

My role: As a co-founder involved in day-to-day delivery, I drafted student service agreements, revised CVs and cover letters, supported job applications, and coordinated application progress. I also participated in reviewing targeting problems and improving the workflow. I did not provide investment capital.

05 / Outcomes & reflection

Service delivery informed better product decisions.

Operational efficiency

Earlier resume-reported workflow metrics include a reduction in weekly spreadsheet maintenance from 5 hours to 1 hour and a 50% reduction in per-client management overhead. These figures require confirmation of the measurement method and individual attribution.

What I learned

The most valuable product decision was not adding more features. It was changing the problem definition—from improving application documents to improving which opportunities were pursued.

Scope and limitations: This portfolio demo is a newly built, simplified interpretation of the workflow. It is not the original internal tool, does not process real applicant data, and does not validate placement outcomes or scoring accuracy.
