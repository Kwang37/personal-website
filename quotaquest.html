01 / A real client signal

300 applications exposed a targeting problem.

An early applicant was doing a lot of work—and receiving almost no response. Reviewing 300 submissions showed that roughly 20% went to employers explicitly excluding international students.

That finding reframed the service. We needed to pay attention to eligibility and fit before volume, rather than treating a polished resume as the only fix for a struggling search.

Insight: Optimizing application materials cannot compensate for targeting roles a candidate cannot pursue.

02 / My day-to-day role

The operational details were the product.

06 / Reflection

The best workflow starts with a better question.

The highest-leverage change was recognizing that the service should not optimize application volume in isolation. Agreements, client communication, tailored materials and applicant eligibility were all part of one customer journey.

My lesson: Product thinking is as relevant to service operations as it is to software. Understand the user’s constraint before automating the wrong behavior.
